# Physics-Constrained Spirometry Extension for PredictX

This repository contains the complete computational research workflow for reconstructing incomplete forced-expiration spirometry and supporting airflow-obstruction screening decisions. It extends PredictX with a bounded physiological inverse model, participant-separated statistical baselines, repeated-manoeuvre evidence, uncertainty-aware selection, and frozen cross-cycle evaluation.

## Research objective

Premature termination of expiration can leave forced vital capacity (FVC) underestimated while forced expiratory volume in one second (FEV1) remains measurable. The resulting FEV1/FVC ratio can shift upward across the fixed 0.70 airflow-obstruction threshold. This project reconstructs the latent terminal volume from the observed volume-time prefix and determines whether the available evidence supports classification or deferral.

## Methodology

The workflow:

1. Decodes public NHANES SPXRAW volume increments at 100 Hz.
2. Detects forced-expiration onset and applies back-extrapolated time-zero correction.
3. Classifies endpoint completeness and acquisition quality.
4. Routes complete manoeuvres to directly measured endpoints.
5. For incomplete manoeuvres, retains measured FEV1 and recovers only the missing FVC with a bounded two-compartment volume-conserving model.
6. Estimates the recovered-ratio calibration from Cycle F TRAIN incomplete cases only.
7. Aggregates acceptable repeated manoeuvres using session-level best-test evidence.
8. Applies boundary-aware uncertainty and selective decision rules.
9. Compares the physics workflow with quality-control-first handling, FEV1/FEV6, direct-ratio selection, state-aware ridge regression, and a physics-plus-ridge hybrid.
10. Evaluates locked rules on participant-disjoint NHANES Cycles E and G.

The routed endpoint table retains the unmodified physiological fit in explicit
`physics_*` audit columns. This makes the state decision testable and prevents a
complete measurement from being silently replaced by an extrapolated endpoint.

## Repository structure

- `predictx/` — signal reconstruction, physiological modeling, clinical-policy gates, robustness studies, and statistical analyses.
- `tools/` — publication-grade audits, frozen cross-cycle validation, baseline evaluation, diagnostic analysis, and manuscript generation.
- `tests/` — parser and nonuniform-grid model tests.
- `results/` — machine-readable summary results and human-readable analytical reports. Large row-level outputs are reproducible and intentionally excluded.
- `figures/` — retained scientific figures generated by the analysis.
- `paper/` — final IEEE-format research paper.

## Data

Raw NHANES files are not committed. Download the public CDC/NCHS files and place them in the following layout:

```text
NHANES_SpiroSBI/
├── 2007-2008/
│   ├── SPX_E.xpt
│   └── spxraw_e.sas7bdat
├── 2009-2010/
│   ├── SPX_F.xpt
│   └── spxraw_f.sas7bdat
└── 2011-2012/
    ├── SPX_G.xpt
    └── spxraw_g.sas7bdat
```

Additional demographic, smoking, examination, and medical-condition releases are resolved by the relevant analysis modules.

## Principal evaluation design

- Controlled censoring uses complete curves with the terminal endpoint hidden from the estimator.
- Natural-pair evaluation compares separate manoeuvres from the same examination.
- Cycle F supplies model-development and policy-development partitions with participant separation.
- Cycles E and G provide locked post-audit cross-cycle re-evaluation. Model selection, ratio calibration, regularization, and operating thresholds use Cycle F TRAIN only.
- Participant bootstrap intervals quantify uncertainty for coverage, sensitivity, specificity, agreement, and endpoint error.

The pre-correction implementation applied modeled endpoints to complete
manoeuvres and also substituted modeled FEV1. The current executable workflow
corrects both routing errors; the machine-readable result records the revision
and the exact Cycle F training provenance.

## Corrected cross-cycle result

Adding physics-recovered FVC as one feature in the Cycle-F-trained ridge hybrid
reduced state-aware FVC mean absolute error relative to ridge alone in both
cross-cycle evaluations. The paired reductions were 1.22 mL in Cycle E (95%
bootstrap interval 0.49-1.92 mL) and 0.96 mL in Cycle G (0.25-1.72 mL). Among
participants with no complete acceptable manoeuvre, the reduction was 11.63 mL
in Cycle E (3.81-19.78 mL) and 7.68 mL in Cycle G (interval -0.29 to 16.15 mL).
The standalone physics workflow and classification comparisons remain reported
separately in the consolidated artifacts.

## Data and artifact policy

The `.gitignore` excludes raw datasets, downloaded archives, caches, temporary render products, virtual environments, and large row-level result tables.
